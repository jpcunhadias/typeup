# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want taking part in typeup to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- being kind and patient, especially with people new to pandoc or LaTeX;
- respecting different opinions and experiences;
- giving and taking constructive feedback gracefully;
- owning mistakes and learning from them.

Examples of behaviour that is not acceptable:

- sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention;
- trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing someone else's private information without their permission;
- anything else that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions)
and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer at
joaopedrocdias@hotmail.com. Every report is reviewed promptly and
fairly, and the reporter's privacy is respected.

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code,
issues and other contributions that break this code of conduct, and may
warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban a contributor.
